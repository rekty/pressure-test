      Decision models answer with a choice and a probability for each option, which makes them unusually honest opponents: this app shows the distribution it sampled from, so a 70% steal is visible as a 70% steal and not as a mystery. The game is the one from the decision benchmark write-ups: cooperate for a small shared payoff, or defect for a bigger one-sided payoff that collapses if both defect. Payoffs here are 3/3, 0/5, 5/0 and 1/1.

      Play and tournament both spend the reader's own Pollen through gen.pollinations.ai/alpha/decisions. Nothing is stored server-side.
